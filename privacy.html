What we do not collect

  • Payment details. The app involves no real money and no payment processing.
  • Bank, brokerage or investment account information. There is no brokerage relationship.
  • Identity documents, National Insurance numbers or financial history.
  • Location data, contacts, photos, or your device's advertising identifier.

We do not use advertising trackers, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or share personal data with third parties for marketing.

Who processes your data

We use Supabase to host the database, authentication and server functions behind the app. Supabase acts as our processor under a data processing agreement, and data is stored in the [REGION] region.

We use Apple's App Store and TestFlight for distribution. Apple provides us with aggregate, anonymised statistics about downloads and crashes. Apple's own handling of your data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we delete your email address, username, portfolio, trades and scores immediately. Technical logs are kept for up to [NUMBER] days and then deleted.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all associated data from inside the app: open the Account tab and select Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent, and you do not need to contact us to do it.

If you would rather we did it for you, email privacy@[YOURDOMAIN] from the address on the account.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to access a copy of your data, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have your data erased, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. To exercise any of these, email privacy@[YOURDOMAIN]. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Children

Moon Trading is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email privacy@[YOURDOMAIN] and we will delete it.

Security

Passwords are hashed, never stored in readable form. Data is encrypted in transit. Database access is restricted per user by row-level security, so one account cannot read another account's portfolio or trade history.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect.
